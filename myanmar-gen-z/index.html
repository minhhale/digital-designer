Edward R. Murrow Award – Digital (2025)

Myanmar Gen Z: Fighting a War for the Future

Battle lines in Myanmar are constantly moving. Rebel forces made up of hundreds of factions have been on the march in the past year, scoring a series of victories against junta forces.

This long-form digital special report explores how Myanmar’s Gen Z is at the frontlines of the resistance against military rule, blending storytelling, interactive design, and visual journalism. The site combines compelling narratives with a highly visual, responsive interface to engage global audiences.

My Role

UX/UI Design

Used Figma to design wireframes and prototypes that structured and enhanced the project’s narrative flow.

Visual Design

Led the visual and experiential design of the project, including layout, typography, graphic design to craft a cohesive, human-centered narrative that engages and resonates with the audience.

Front-End Development

Built with HTML, CSS, Gsap, and JavaScript for a smooth reading experience.

Accessibility Compliance

Applied WCAG guidelines for screen readers, color contrast, and responsive scaling

The Challenge

The editorial team needed a way to translate deeply complex reporting into a digital experience that could hold readers’ attention while respecting the sensitivity of the subject matter. The challenge was to balance immersive storytelling with clarity, accessibility, and emotional impact.

The Process

1. Research & Planning
  • • Reviewed RFA’s editorial goals and audience needs.
  • • Mapped out content flow with editors to highlight the human stories alongside the political context.
  • • Created low-fidelity wireframes to define narrative pacing (intro → personal stories → conflict → call to reflection).
2. Design & Prototyping
  • • Developed a scroll-based storytelling layout inspired by digital longform journalism.
  • • Chose typography and color palette to balance seriousness (dark, muted tones) with urgency (highlight color accents).
  • • Integrated large hero images and section dividers to create emotional impact.
3. Accessibility & Responsiveness
  • • Implemented alt text and semantic HTML for screen reader compatibility.
  • • Designed for breakpoints (desktop, tablet, mobile) to ensure seamless reading across devices.
  • • Applied WCAG contrast standards for legibility.

Visual Design Rationale

Goal: Capture the attention of Gen Z readers by combining real human stories, immersive environmental context, and modern visual techniques.

  • • Real cut-out portraits place authentic personalities at the forefront, creating empathy, focus, and human connection beyond generic stock imagery. These portraits are then enhanced with AI (Midjourney) to achieve a modern, cohesive visual style.
  • • Real jungle imagery, stylized with AI (e.g. Midjourney), provides context, atmosphere, and visual richness—raw but modern, textured, cinematic.
  • • Scroll-trigger interactions (parallax, reveal, fade, transitions) sustain interest, guide the narrative flow, and deliver an immersive reading experience.

The new design balances emotional impact with modern aesthetics to engage a global audience.

Before

The original banner featured real photos of Gen Z protesters, but the cluttered backgrounds and static layout made it difficult for viewers to focus on the individuals and their expressions.

The busy visual environment distracted from the emotional impact of the story, and the lack of interactivity resulted in a passive reading experience.

After

To address this, I redesigned the banner using cut-out portraits, AI-enhanced imagery, and scroll-trigger interactions, creating a modern, visually cohesive, and immersive experience that draws attention to the individuals and their stories while guiding readers through the narrative.

Result

The redesign transforms a static news page into an interactive, Gen Z–friendly experience that feels authentic, modern, and emotionally resonant. Scroll-triggered animations and parallax effects create a dynamic storytelling experience that draws readers in.

Created collages depicting Myanmar Gen Z first as civilians and then as soldiers, using scroll-trigger animations to visually narrate their transformation in an immersive, interactive way.

The Solution

The final website delivers:

- A cinematic scrolling experience with high-impact photography.

- Chapter-based storytelling that guides readers through a structured narrative.

- Interactive elements (timelines, callouts, visual markers) to keep readers engaged.

- A responsive, mobile-first design ensuring accessibility across devices.

The Impact
🏆

- Won the 2025 Edward R. Murrow Awards Digital for excellence in multimedia journalism.

- Garnered global recognition for effectively amplifying Gen Z voices in Myanmar.

- Increased audience engagement through time-on-page metrics compared to traditional article formats.

- Strengthened the newsroom’s reputation for innovation in digital storytelling.
